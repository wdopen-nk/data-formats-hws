1. Overview

This model describes the car dealership network. In particular it describes the dealership, the customers who buy cars from the dealer, the workers who are employed in this dealership, and the models of cars the cars belong to

2. Classes

Class: Dealership
Description: A business that sells cars to customers. It operates at a physical address, employs staff and holds cars.
Attributes:
- name: String - name under which the dealership operates.
- address: String - physical address of the dealership, e.g Patkova 2136/3 182 00 Praha 8.
- foundedDate: Date - date on which the dealership was founded
  Associations:
- full-time employee (1..1 Dealership) <-> (0..* Employee)
- regular customer (0..* Dealership) <-> (0..* Customer)
- dealer (0..1 Dealership) <-> (0..* Car)

Class: Person
Description: Class representing a person related to the dealership, either as an employee or as a customer.
Attributes:
- firstName: String - given name of the person.
- lastName: String - family name of the person.
- phoneNumber: String [0..*] - phone numbers of the particular person e.g. +420 123 456 789. A person may have none or several.
Associations:
- none directly.

Class: Employee
Superclass: Person
Description: Represents individuals working full-time at a dealership
e.g as salespersons or mechanics.
Attributes:
- inherited from Person (firstName, lastName, phoneNumber), no attributes
  on its own.
Associations:
- full-time employee (0..* Employee) <-> (1..1 Dealership)

Class: Customer
Superclass: Person
Description: Represents person who buy cars from dealerships.
Attributes:
- inherited from Person (firstName, lastName, phoneNumber), no attributes
  on its own.
  Associations:
- regular customer (0..* Customer) <-> (0..* Dealership)
- buyer (0..1 Customer) <-> (0..* Car)

Class: Car
Description: Represents a physical vehicle that is carried and
sold by a dealership.
Attributes:
- color: String - exterior color of the car's body.
- manufacturedDate: Date - date on which the car was manufactured.
- vin: Integer - Vehicle Identification Number, used to identify a car’s 
manufacturer, origin, and specifications.
  Associations:
- dealer (0..* Car) <-> (0..1 Dealership)
- buyer (0..* Car) <-> (0..1 Customer)
- model (0..* Car) <-> (1..1 CarModel)

Class: CarModel
Description: Represents a type of car sold under a particular name,
shared by all cars built according to it.
Attributes:
- modelName: String - name under which the model is sold e.g Camry.
- debutDate: Date - date on which the model was first presented.
- enginePower: Integer - maximum engine power in kilowatts (kW).
  Associations:
- model (1..1 CarModel) <-> (0..* Car)

Generalization
- Employee -> Person
- Customer -> Person
